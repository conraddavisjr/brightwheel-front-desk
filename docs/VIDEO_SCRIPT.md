# Video script (under 2 minutes)

Record on a phone-sized browser window for the parent half, then a desktop window for the director half.
Speak in your own words; this is a guide, about 260 words.

**0:00 - 0:12 · The problem**
"Directors answer the same questions all day: hours, tuition, is my kid too sick to come in.
The answer is in a handbook somewhere, and sometimes the handbook is wrong.
This is Front Desk."

**0:12 - 0:45 · Parent**
(Harbor Kids parent.) Tap the mic: "Is my son too sick to come in with a fever?"
"It says one moment instantly, answers from the school's current policy, and cites it.
This school has two illness policies that disagree; it uses the new one and flags the old one to the director."
Ask "When is picture day?" and tap Add to Google Calendar.
Ask "What's the alarm code?": "The code is in their docs. Privacy Guard kept it out."

**0:45 - 1:05 · Uncertainty**
Ask "Do you offer a summer camp?"
"It doesn't guess.
It says it doesn't know and sends the question to the front desk."

**1:05 - 1:40 · Director**
Switch to the director. Home: "This is the value: answered without staff, hours saved, what families ask."
Inbox: answer the summer camp question in one sentence, then Publish.
Privacy Guard: "Every document is checked before parents can search it.
Here's the alarm code, a staff member's cell, a child's allergy note.
I decide: redact, staff only, or fine."
Tap + to add a rule; it scans everything.

**1:40 - 1:55 · Close the loop**
Back on the phone, ask about summer camp again: cited, from the director's answer.
"Answer once, and it's handled for every family after."

**1:55 - 2:00**
"Built with Next.js, Postgres hybrid search, Gemini on the free tier, and tested with evals.
Details are in the README."

Directors answer the same questions all day, by phone, by text and at pickup. Front Desk answers families instantly from the school's own documents, says plainly when it can't, and turns every gap into something the director fixes once, for every family after.

What it does

For families

  • Ask by text, voice or a guided "Can my child come in today?" check.
  • One direct answer with its source: hours, tuition tables, policy steps, events with Add to Calendar.
  • Anything uncertain goes to staff, and their reply lands on the parent's home.

For directors

  • Connect Notion, Google Drive and Calendar (real, public demo workspaces).
  • An Inbox of unanswered questions and conflicting documents: answer once, done for everyone.
  • Privacy Guard holds codes, payroll, custody and child health before families can search them.

How it meets the brief

Realistic scope, finishedTwo schools: a tidy Notion handbook, and a deliberately messy Notion, Drive and Calendar mix with real conflicts and secrets. Both sides work end to end, deployed and mobile-first.
Hands-on executionBuilt, tested and shipped end to end: 144 end-to-end journeys on desktop and phone, 107 unit tests, zero accessibility violations, and CI that fails on any eval gate.
Customer judgmentThe answer comes first, in one sentence. One tap reaches a person. Internal problems stay internal: document conflicts go to the director, never to a parent.
AI judgmentThree checks before any answer: retrieval strength, the model's own grounded flag, and a real citation. Failing one gives an honest "I don't have that yet" and an Inbox item, never a guess.
Technical judgmentHybrid search (pgvector plus full text, fused with RRF) in Postgres. Rules run before any model: 911 first, injections refused. Every field is scanned before a parent sees it. Row-level security per school. $0 to run: Gemini free tier, a hard daily cap, an offline fallback.
Operator controlEdit the knowledge and the hours families see, add or toggle privacy rules, redact or allow with undo, and see what families ask and where answers fell short.

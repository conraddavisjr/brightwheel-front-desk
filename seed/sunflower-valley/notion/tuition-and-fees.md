# Tuition & Fees

All rates below are for the 2026-27 school year (August 2026 through August 2027).
Tuition is billed monthly and is the same each month regardless of holidays, closures, illness, or vacation.

## Monthly Tuition

| Classroom | Ages | Full Time (5 days) | Part Time (3 days, M/W/F) | Part Time (2 days, T/Th) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seedlings (Infants) | 6 weeks - 12 months | $2,650 | $1,825 | Not offered |
| Sprouts (Young Toddlers) | 12 - 24 months | $2,395 | $1,660 | $1,195 |
| Saplings (Twos) | 2 - 3 years | $2,150 | $1,495 | $1,075 |
| Blossoms (Preschool) | 3 - 4 years | $1,875 | $1,310 | $940 |
| Sunbeams (Pre-K) | 4 - 5 years | $1,825 | $1,275 | $915 |

Part-time days are fixed.
Swapping days is not possible because our classrooms are staffed to the enrolled schedule.

## One-Time and Annual Fees

| Fee | Amount | When it is charged |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application fee | $75 | When you join the waitlist (non-refundable) |
| Registration fee | $150 | When you accept an enrollment offer (non-refundable) |
| Enrollment deposit | Equal to one month of tuition | When you accept an offer, applied to your final month |
| Annual materials fee | $250 | Each August, or prorated if enrolling mid-year |

## Sibling Discount

Families with two or more children enrolled at the same time receive **10% off the tuition of the older child** (or children).
The youngest child pays the full rate.
The discount applies automatically in brightwheel once both children are enrolled.

## Late Pickup Fee

The school closes at 6:00 PM.
A late pickup fee of **$1 per minute** begins at 6:01 PM and is charged to your brightwheel account.
See Drop-off & Pickup for details.

## Paying Tuition

### Autopay via brightwheel

All families pay through **brightwheel autopay**.

1. Open the brightwheel app and go to **Billing**.
2. Add a bank account (ACH) or a debit or credit card.
3. Turn on **Autopay**.

Tuition is drafted automatically on the **1st of each month**.
ACH payments have no processing fee.
Card payments include the processing fee shown in the app.

### Late Payments

- Payments not received by the **5th** of the month incur a **$35 late fee**.
- If tuition is more than 15 days past due, we will reach out to set up a plan. Care may be paused if an account is more than 30 days past due.

## Withdrawal

We require **30 days written notice** before withdrawing your child.
Notice can be submitted by messaging the front desk in brightwheel.
Your enrollment deposit is applied to your final month of tuition when proper notice is given.

## Tax Documents and FSA

- Our federal tax ID and a year-end payment statement are available in brightwheel under **Billing > Statements** each January.
- Many families use a Dependent Care FSA. We are happy to sign FSA claim forms; please allow 3 business days.

## Subsidies

We accept subsidized care through the Alameda County child care resource and referral programs.
Please talk with our Assistant Director, Daniel Whitfield, about eligibility and paperwork.

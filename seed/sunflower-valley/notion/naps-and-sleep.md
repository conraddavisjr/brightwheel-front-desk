# Naps & Sleep

Rest is essential for growing bodies and brains.
Our approach changes with your child's age.

## Infant Safe Sleep (Seedlings)

We follow California licensing requirements and **American Academy of Pediatrics safe sleep guidelines** for every infant, every time.

- Infants are always placed to sleep **on their backs**, in a **crib** that meets federal safety standards.
- Each infant has their own crib with a **firm, tight-fitting mattress** and a **fitted sheet only**.
- **Nothing else goes in the crib:** no blankets, pillows, bumpers, stuffed animals, or positioning devices.
- **No swaddling** in the Seedlings room. Sleep sacks (wearable blankets) without weighted parts are welcome.
- Infants who fall asleep anywhere other than a crib (in a bouncer, in arms, in a stroller) are moved to their crib right away.
- If an infant rolls over on their own, they may stay in the position they choose once they can roll both ways.
- Teachers do **visual checks every 15 minutes** and record them in brightwheel.
- Pacifiers are welcome but are not attached to clothing or to the crib.
- Infants follow their own sleep schedules. We never wake a sleeping baby except as you request in their care plan.

If your child's health care provider requires a different sleep position for a medical reason, we need a **signed waiver from the provider** before we can follow it.

## Toddlers (Sprouts and Saplings)

- Children nap on individual **cots or mats**, labeled with their name, spaced at least 3 feet apart.
- Rest time is about **12:00 to 2:30 PM**.
- Families send a small blanket and a comfort item. Blankets go home every Friday for washing.
- Teachers stay in the room for the whole rest period with lights dimmed and soft music.

## Preschool and Pre-K (Blossoms and Sunbeams)

- Rest time is **12:30 to 2:00 PM**.
- Children rest on cots. Those who do not fall asleep after 45 minutes may get up for **quiet activities** (books, puzzles, drawing).
- We do not force children to sleep, and we do not keep children from sleeping.

## Can I ask you to shorten my child's nap?

We understand that naps can affect bedtime.
We will try to honor requests, but we will not wake a sleeping child in a way that disrupts the rest of the room, and California licensing does not allow us to prevent a child from resting if they are tired.
Let's talk about it together.

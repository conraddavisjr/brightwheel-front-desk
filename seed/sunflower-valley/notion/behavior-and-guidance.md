# Behavior & Guidance

Young children are still learning how to manage big feelings, share, and solve problems.
Our job is to teach, not punish.
We use **positive guidance** grounded in the Pyramid Model for Supporting Social Emotional Competence.

## What We Do

- **Set up for success.** Predictable routines, enough materials, and engaging activities prevent many conflicts.
- **Name feelings.** "You're frustrated that the tower fell. That's hard."
- **Set clear, simple limits.** "I won't let you hit. Hitting hurts."
- **Offer choices.** "You can walk to the sink or hop like a bunny."
- **Teach problem solving.** Older children use our "solution kit" picture cards to find a fair answer together.
- **Redirect.** Toddlers are guided to a different activity when needed.
- **Calm-down space.** Every classroom has a cozy spot children can choose when they need a break. It is never used as punishment.

## What We Never Do

- We never use physical punishment, shaming, threats, or yelling.
- We never withhold food, rest, or outdoor time as a consequence.
- We never use isolation or "time out" away from adult supervision.

## Biting

Biting is very common in toddlers and usually happens because of teething, frustration, or limited language.

- We care for the child who was bitten first, then calmly tell the child who bit, "Biting hurts. Teeth are for food."
- Both families are notified the same day through an incident report.
- **We do not share the name of the other child** involved in any incident, including biting. This protects every child's privacy.
- If biting continues, we create a plan with the family, including extra supervision and teething supports.

## When We Need Extra Support

If a child's behavior is persistently unsafe for themselves or others, we will:

1. Meet with the family to share observations and hear what is happening at home.
2. Create a written support plan together.
3. With family permission, connect with outside resources such as an early intervention specialist or a mental health consultant through the Alameda County Help Me Grow program.

Dismissal is a last resort, and only after we have tried a support plan together.

## Children with Disabilities

We welcome children with disabilities and developmental differences and will make reasonable accommodations in line with the Americans with Disabilities Act.
We are glad to coordinate with your child's IFSP or IEP team.

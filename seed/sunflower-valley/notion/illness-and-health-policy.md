# Illness & Health Policy

Keeping children healthy is a team effort.
Our policy follows guidance from the California Department of Social Services, the American Academy of Pediatrics, and Alameda County Public Health.
When in doubt, please keep your child home and message us.

## Daily Health Check

A teacher greets each child at drop-off and does a quick visual health check.
If your child seems unwell at arrival, we may ask you to take them home.

## When Your Child Must Stay Home

Please keep your child home, and expect a call to pick up within **one hour**, if they have any of the following.

### Fever

- A temperature of **100.4°F (38.0°C) or higher**.
- Children may return once they have been **fever-free for 24 hours without fever-reducing medication** (such as acetaminophen or ibuprofen).
- Example: if your child has a fever on Tuesday afternoon and no fever without medicine through Wednesday afternoon, they may return Thursday morning.

### Vomiting

- Two or more episodes of vomiting in 24 hours, or one episode with other signs of illness.
- Children may return **24 hours after the last episode** and once they can keep down a normal diet.

### Diarrhea

- Two or more loose stools beyond what is normal for your child in one day, or any diarrhea with blood or mucus.
- For diapered children, any diarrhea that cannot be contained in the diaper.
- Children may return **24 hours after the last loose stool**.

### Conjunctivitis (Pink Eye)

- Eye redness with yellow or green discharge, crusting, or matting.
- Children may return once they have been evaluated by a health care provider and either started treatment or received a note saying they may attend.
- Clear, watery eyes without other symptoms do not require exclusion.

### Rash

- A rash with fever or behavior change, or any rash that is spreading quickly or blistering.
- Children may return with a note from a health care provider saying the rash is not contagious.

### Head Lice

- If live lice are found, we will call you so your child can be picked up at the end of the day. They may finish the day with hair tied up and limited head-to-head contact.
- Children may return after the first treatment.
- We will send a general notice to the classroom without naming your child.

### Other Conditions

- Strep throat: may return after 12 hours on antibiotics and fever-free.
- Hand, foot, and mouth disease: may return when fever-free and sores are not weeping.
- COVID-19 and influenza: we follow current Alameda County Public Health guidance and will post any changes in brightwheel.
- Any illness that keeps your child from participating comfortably or that needs more care than teachers can give without compromising care for the group.

## Notifying Us

If your child is absent due to illness, please mark them absent in brightwheel and share the symptoms.
We track communicable illness and notify classroom families (without names) when there is a confirmed case, as required by licensing.

## Medication

We can give medication at school only with a completed **Medication Authorization Form**.

- Prescription medication must be in the **original pharmacy container** with your child's name, dose, and instructions.
- Over-the-counter medication requires the form **and** written instructions from your child's health care provider if the dose differs from the label.
- We do not give the first dose of any new medication at school, in case of a reaction.
- Medication is stored in a locked box in each classroom, or refrigerated as needed.
- Each dose is logged in brightwheel so you can see the time and amount.

### Emergency Medication

Children with an **EpiPen, inhaler, or other emergency medication** must have an **Individual Health Care Plan** signed by their health care provider and parent.
Emergency medication travels with the class on walks and during drills.

### Diaper Creams and Sunscreen

Diaper cream and sunscreen require a one-time signed permission in brightwheel.
Please label each tube with your child's name.

## Immunizations

California law requires that children in child care be up to date on immunizations or have a valid medical exemption on file.
Please upload your child's updated immunization record (the yellow card or a California Immunization Registry printout) after each well-child visit.

## Questions

Health questions can go to our Assistant Director, Daniel Whitfield, through brightwheel.
We are not able to give medical advice, so please contact your child's health care provider with medical concerns.

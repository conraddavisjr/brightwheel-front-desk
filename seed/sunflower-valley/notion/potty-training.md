# Potty Training

We call it **toilet learning**, and we approach it as a partnership with families.
Every child is different, and there is no "right" age.
Most children show readiness between 2 and 3 1/2 years old.

## Signs of Readiness

- Stays dry for 2 hours or more, or wakes up dry from nap
- Shows interest in the bathroom or in wearing underwear
- Tells you (with words or gestures) when their diaper is wet or soiled
- Can pull pants up and down with some help
- Can follow simple two-step directions

## How We Work Together

1. **Talk with your child's teacher** when you see signs of readiness at home. We will share what we are seeing at school too.
2. **Start at home first**, ideally over a long weekend, so your child has a consistent routine.
3. **We follow your lead at school.** We will offer the toilet on a regular schedule (on arrival, before and after meals, before and after nap, before outdoor play) and whenever your child asks.
4. **We use positive language.** We cheer for trying, not just success, and never shame accidents.

## What to Send

- **Five or more full changes** of clothes, including socks and shoes if possible
- Underwear (if your child is wearing it)
- Pull-ups for nap time, if you prefer
- Clothes that are easy for your child to manage: elastic waist pants, no overalls or belts

Wet or soiled clothing is sent home in a sealed bag.

## Accidents

Accidents are a normal part of learning.
Teachers will help your child clean up and change in a calm, matter-of-fact way.

## Enrollment Requirements

Children do **not** need to be potty trained to move up to Blossoms (preschool).
Children in Sunbeams (pre-K) are typically toilet trained, but we will support any child who is still learning.

# Safety & Emergencies

We plan and practice for emergencies so that, if one happens, everyone knows what to do.
This page describes our approach at a policy level.
For the safety of children and staff, specific security details are not shared publicly.

## Building Security

- The front entrance stays locked at all times. Families enter using their brightwheel check-in, and visitors are let in by the front desk.
- All visitors sign in, show ID, and wear a visitor badge.
- Play yards are fully fenced, and gates have child-proof latches.
- Classroom doors to the outside are monitored, and children are counted at every transition.

## Drills

We practice regularly, as required by California licensing:

| Drill | How often |
| --- | --- |
| Fire evacuation | Monthly |
| Earthquake (drop, cover, hold on) | Quarterly |
| Shelter-in-place | Twice a year |
| Lockdown | Twice a year |

Infants are evacuated in our multi-seat evacuation cribs.
Drills are practiced calmly, with age-appropriate explanations so children are not frightened.

## Emergency Procedures

### Evacuation

If we must leave the building, children and staff will gather at our **primary assembly area** in the play yard.
If the area around the school is unsafe, we will walk to our **off-site relocation site**, a nearby partner location.
The name of the relocation site is shared with enrolled families in brightwheel and posted inside the building.

### Shelter-in-Place

For outdoor hazards such as poor air quality or a gas leak nearby, we will keep children indoors with windows and doors closed and HVAC adjusted.

### Lockdown

If there is a threat near the school, staff will secure classrooms and keep children calm and quiet until police give the all-clear.

### Earthquake

We keep **72 hours of emergency supplies** on site: water, food, first aid kits, flashlights, blankets, diapers, formula, and each child's comfort photo from home.

## How We Communicate in an Emergency

- We will send an alert through **brightwheel** (push notification, text, and email) as soon as children are safe.
- Please **do not come to the school** during an active emergency unless we ask you to. Roads must stay clear for emergency vehicles.
- We will tell you where and when to reunite with your child.
- Children are only released to authorized pickups, with ID, during reunification.

## First Aid and Injuries

- All lead teachers hold current **pediatric First Aid and CPR** certification.
- Minor injuries (a scraped knee, a small bump) are recorded in an incident report in brightwheel, and you will be notified the same day.
- Any **head injury**, however small, results in a phone call to you.
- For serious injuries we call 911 first and then you.

## Mandated Reporting

All staff are mandated reporters under California law.
We are required to report any reasonable suspicion of child abuse or neglect to Child Protective Services.

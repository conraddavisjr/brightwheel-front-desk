# Daily Routine

Predictable routines help young children feel secure.
The schedules below are samples, and teachers adjust them to the children's needs, the weather, and the projects underway.

## Seedlings (Infants, 6 weeks - 12 months)

Infants follow their **own individual schedules** for eating, sleeping, and diapering.
Your child's daily report in brightwheel shows each bottle, nap, and diaper.

A typical day includes:

- Arrival and greeting, warm handoff with your child's primary caregiver
- Tummy time, floor play, and sensory exploration
- Outdoor time in our infant garden or a buggy walk
- Songs, books, and lap time
- Bottles and solids on demand, following your feeding plan
- Naps whenever your baby is tired, always in a crib on their back

## What to Bring

- Two full changes of clothes (labeled), including socks
- Weather-appropriate outdoor clothing and a rain jacket
- A labeled water bottle (2 years and up)
- A small blanket and comfort item for nap
- Diapers and wipes (Seedlings, Sprouts, and Saplings, unless potty trained)

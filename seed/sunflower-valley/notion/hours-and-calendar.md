# Hours & Calendar

## Daily Hours

Sunflower Valley is open **Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM**.

- Doors open for drop-off at 7:00 AM.
- Our core learning day runs from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. We ask that children arrive by 9:00 AM so they can join morning meeting.
- The building closes at 6:00 PM. Late pickup fees begin at 6:01 PM (see Drop-off & Pickup).
- The front desk is staffed from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

We are closed on weekends and on the holidays and closure days listed below.

## Unplanned Closures

In rare cases, we may close because of a power outage, poor air quality, or another emergency.

- **Air quality:** During wildfire season, we monitor the AQI for Oakland. Above 150, children stay indoors with air purifiers running. Above 300, we may close or dismiss early.
- **Power or water outage:** If we cannot safely operate, we will notify families through brightwheel and ask for pickup within one hour.
- **Notification:** All closure announcements go out by brightwheel push notification, email, and text.

# Drop-off & Pickup

## Signing In and Out

California licensing requires a **full signature or electronic check-in** for every arrival and departure.
We use **brightwheel check-in**:

1. Open brightwheel and tap **Check In** (or **Check Out**).
2. Scan the QR code at the front desk or enter your 4-digit family check-in PIN at the kiosk.
3. Walk your child to their classroom and hand off to a teacher.

Please never leave your child at the door or in the hallway.
A teacher must see your child arrive.

## Drop-off

- Drop-off begins at **7:00 AM**.
- Please arrive by **9:00 AM** so your child can join morning meeting.
- If your child will arrive after 9:00 AM because of an appointment, message us in brightwheel by 8:30 AM.
- Short, warm goodbyes help children settle. Teachers will send a photo or message if your child needs comfort after you leave.

## Parking

Short-term parking is available in the five spots along the school side of Linden Grove Avenue (15-minute limit, 7:00-9:30 AM and 4:00-6:00 PM).
Please do not park in the driveway of our neighbors at 4116 Linden Grove.

## Authorized Pickup

For your child's safety, we only release children to adults listed as **authorized pickups** in brightwheel.

- Parents can add or remove authorized pickups in brightwheel at any time.
- Everyone picking up must be **18 or older** and show a **government-issued photo ID** the first several times they pick up, and any time a staff member does not recognize them.
- If someone not on the list arrives, we will not release your child. We will call you.
- For a one-time pickup by someone new, add them in brightwheel before they arrive and let the front desk know.

### Custody and Court Orders

If there is a court order limiting a parent's or guardian's access to a child, we must have a **copy of the current court order** on file.
Without a court order, we are required by law to release a child to either legal parent.
Court documents are stored securely in your child's confidential file and shared only with the Director and the classroom lead teacher.

### Safety Concerns at Pickup

Staff will not release a child to any adult who appears impaired by alcohol or drugs.
In that situation we will ask the adult to call another authorized pickup, and we may contact emergency services if the adult insists on leaving with the child.

## Late Pickup

We close at **6:00 PM**.

- If you are running late, please call the front desk at (510) 555-0147 so we can reassure your child.
- A late fee of **$1 per minute** begins at **6:01 PM**, based on the brightwheel check-out time.
- Late fees are added to your next brightwheel invoice.
- After **three late pickups in one school year**, we will schedule a meeting to make a plan together.
- If a child has not been picked up by **6:30 PM** and we cannot reach any parent or emergency contact, we are required to contact the Oakland Police Department and Child Protective Services.

## Early Pickup

If you plan to pick up early (for example, during nap time), please message the teacher so your child can be ready.
We ask that early pickups avoid the middle of rest time (12:30 - 2:00 PM) when possible.

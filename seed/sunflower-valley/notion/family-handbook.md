# Family Handbook

## Our Mission

We believe children learn best when they feel safe, known, and free to explore.
Our mission is to partner with families to nurture curious, kind, and confident children through play-based learning, warm relationships, and a deep respect for each child's pace.

## Our Philosophy

Our curriculum is play-based and emergent, drawing from Reggio Emilia-inspired practices and the California Preschool Learning Foundations.
Teachers observe what children are curious about and build projects around those interests.
A week in the Blossoms room might start with a question about why worms come up after rain and turn into a garden investigation, a class book, and a worm hotel.

We value:

- **Relationships first.** Each child has a primary caregiver who knows their cues, routines, and favorite songs.
- **Outdoor time every day.** Our garden and two play yards are part of the classroom, rain or shine.
- **Families as partners.** You know your child best, and we want to hear from you.
- **Inclusion.** We welcome families of every background, language, structure, and ability.

# Staff & Classrooms

## Our Classrooms

| Classroom | Ages | Group size | Teacher to child ratio | California minimum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seedlings | 6 weeks - 12 months | 8 | 1:3 | 1:4 |
| Sprouts | 12 - 24 months | 10 | 1:4 | 1:4 (infant) |
| Saplings | 2 - 3 years | 12 | 1:5 | 1:6 (toddler option) |
| Blossoms | 3 - 4 years | 20 | 1:8 | 1:12 |
| Sunbeams | 4 - 5 years | 24 | 1:8 | 1:12 |

We staff **better than state minimum ratios** at all times, including during breaks and at the beginning and end of the day.
Children move up to the next classroom based on age and developmental readiness, usually in August or January.

## Staff Qualifications

- All teaching staff meet or exceed **California Title 22** education requirements.
- All staff complete a **Live Scan background check** (DOJ and FBI) and a Child Abuse Central Index check before working with children.
- All lead teachers hold current **pediatric First Aid and CPR** certification.
- All staff complete **mandated reporter** training annually.
- Teachers complete at least **24 hours of professional development** each year.

## Primary Caregiving

In Seedlings and Sprouts, each child is assigned a **primary caregiver** who handles most feedings, diapering, and naps, and who is your main point of contact.
This builds secure attachment and a consistent routine.

## Substitute Staff

When a teacher is out, we use our own trained floaters first.
Substitutes are always background cleared and are never left alone as the only adult with a group of children until they are familiar with the classroom.

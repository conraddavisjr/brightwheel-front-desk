# Parent Involvement & Events

Sunflower Valley is a community.
There are lots of ways to get involved, whatever your schedule.

## Ways to Get Involved

- **Classroom visits.** Share a family tradition, read a story in your home language, or show us what you do at work. Schedule with your child's teacher.
- **Garden crew.** Help with our monthly Saturday garden mornings (second Saturday, 9:00 - 11:00 AM).
- **Family Advisory Group.** A small group of parents meets with the Director four times a year to share feedback and help plan events.
- **Wish lists.** Each classroom posts a wish list of books and materials in brightwheel.
- **Teacher appreciation.** Our families organize a week of appreciation each May.

Volunteers who will be with children regularly (more than occasional visits) need a TB clearance and must follow our volunteer guidelines.

## Fall Family Picnic

Join us at **Dimond Park** on **Saturday, October 24, 2026, from 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM**.
We will provide lemonade, water, and fruit.
Families are invited to bring a picnic lunch (remember: nut-free, please) and a blanket.
There will be bubbles, chalk, a parachute game, and a story circle at noon.
Siblings and grandparents are welcome.
If it rains, we will move to the school's play yard and multipurpose room.

## Family Conferences

Conferences are 20-minute meetings with your child's lead teacher to discuss development, goals, and any questions.
Sign up for a time in brightwheel when the sign-up opens about two weeks ahead.

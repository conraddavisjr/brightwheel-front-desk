# Meals & Nutrition

Sunflower Valley participates in the **Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP)**, a USDA program that helps us serve balanced meals and snacks.
Meals are included in tuition.

## What We Serve

Children ages 12 months and older receive:

- **Breakfast** (8:00 - 8:30 AM)
- **Lunch** (11:30 AM - 12:15 PM)
- **Afternoon snack** (3:00 - 3:30 PM)

Meals are prepared on-site by our cook, Rosa Delgado.
We serve whole grains, fresh fruits and vegetables daily, lean proteins, and unflavored milk (whole milk for children 12-24 months, 1% milk for children 2 and older).
We do not serve juice, fried foods, or added sugar desserts.

## Allergy-Aware Practices

### Nut-Free Campus

Sunflower Valley is a **nut-free campus**.
Please do not send any food containing peanuts or tree nuts, including in birthday treats or lunchbox items for field trips.
We use sunflower seed butter instead of peanut butter.

### Food Allergies and Special Diets

- Families must submit a **Food Allergy Action Plan** signed by a health care provider for any food allergy.
- Allergy plans are posted (with family permission) in the classroom and kitchen, and teachers are trained on each child's plan.
- We accommodate religious, cultural, and vegetarian diets. Please share these at enrollment.
- If a medical diet requires substitutions we cannot provide, families may send food from home with a provider's note.

## Infant Feeding (Seedlings)

- Families provide **breast milk or formula** and any special bottles.
  - Breast milk should arrive in labeled bottles or bags with your child's name and the date expressed.
  - Formula should arrive as unopened powder in its original container, labeled with your child's name. Teachers prepare bottles using the instructions on the container.
- We follow each infant's individual feeding plan and feed on demand.
- A quiet, comfortable nursing chair is available in the Seedlings room for parents who want to nurse during the day.
- **Solid foods** are introduced in partnership with families. We provide CACFP-approved infant foods once your child is developmentally ready and you have introduced each food at home first.

## Celebrations

We celebrate birthdays with songs and a special job for the day.
Instead of food treats, families are welcome to donate a book to the classroom library in their child's name.

## Water

Water is available to children throughout the day, indoors and outdoors.
Please send a labeled, refillable water bottle for children 2 and older.

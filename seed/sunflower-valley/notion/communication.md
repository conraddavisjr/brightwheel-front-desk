# Communication

Open, regular communication helps us care for your child together.
Our main communication tool is the **brightwheel** app.

## Getting Started with brightwheel

You will receive an invitation email when your child is enrolled.
Download brightwheel on iOS or Android and sign in with the email address you gave us.
Each parent or guardian can have their own login.

## Messaging

- Message your child's teachers or the front desk directly in brightwheel.
- Teachers check messages during the day but are focused on the children, so replies may take a few hours. The front desk typically replies within 1 business hour.
- For anything urgent (you are running late, your child had a hard night, a change in pickup), **please call** (510) 555-0147.
- Messages sent after 6:00 PM or on weekends will be answered the next business day.

## Daily Reports

Every day you will see real-time updates in brightwheel:

- Check-in and check-out times
- Meals and how much your child ate
- Bottles (Seedlings)
- Naps, with start and end times
- Diapers and bathroom visits
- Activities and learning moments
- Medication given (if any)

## Photos and Videos

Teachers share photos and short videos of your child throughout the week.

- Photos with other children in them are only shared with families whose children are in the photo.
- You choose your photo and media preferences in the enrollment packet. You can change them at any time by messaging the front desk.
- We never post photos of children on public social media without separate written permission.
- Please do not post photos that include other children on social media.

## Newsletters and Announcements

- **Weekly classroom notes** go out every Friday with highlights and the next week's menu.
- **Monthly school newsletter** is sent the first week of each month.
- **School-wide announcements** (closures, events, policy updates) are sent through brightwheel and email.

## Conferences

Family conferences are held twice a year, in **November** and **May**.
You can also request a meeting with your child's teacher at any time.

## Concerns and Feedback

We want to hear from you.

1. Start with your child's lead teacher for classroom questions.
2. Contact the Assistant Director, Daniel Whitfield, for anything unresolved.
3. Contact the Director, Marisol Okafor, at any time.

Families also have the right to contact **California Community Care Licensing** (Oakland Regional Office) with any concerns about the center.

## Language Access

Many of our staff speak Spanish, and we can arrange interpretation for Cantonese, Vietnamese, and other languages for conferences and important meetings.

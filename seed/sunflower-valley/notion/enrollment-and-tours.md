# Enrollment & Tours

We would love to meet you.
Touring is the best way to see our classrooms in action and ask questions.

## Tours

Tours are offered **every Tuesday and Thursday at 9:30 AM and 3:30 PM**.
Each tour lasts about 45 minutes and is led by our Director, Marisol Okafor, or Assistant Director, Daniel Whitfield.

- Tours are small (up to 4 families) so we do not disrupt the children's day.
- Children are welcome to join you.
- No tours are held on school holidays or closure days (see Hours & Calendar).

### How to Book a Tour

1. Visit our enrollment page in brightwheel at **sunflowervalley.example.com/tour**, or message the front desk.
2. Choose an open Tuesday or Thursday slot.
3. You will get a confirmation email and a reminder the day before.

If none of the times work for you, message us and we will do our best to find another time.
Virtual tours over video are available for families relocating to the Bay Area.

## Waitlist

Openings are limited, especially in Seedlings (infants).
After your tour, you can join the waitlist.

- Join through the enrollment link in brightwheel. There is a **$75 non-refundable application fee**.
- Typical wait times: Seedlings 9-12 months, Sprouts 6-9 months, Saplings 4-6 months, Blossoms and Sunbeams 2-4 months.
- Priority order: (1) siblings of currently enrolled children, (2) children of Sunflower Valley staff, (3) all other families by application date.
- We contact families when a spot opens. You will have **5 business days** to accept.
- You can check your place on the waitlist at any time by messaging the front desk.

## Accepting an Enrollment Offer

To hold your spot you will need to:

- Pay the **$150 registration fee** and the **enrollment deposit** (one month of tuition).
- Sign the enrollment agreement in brightwheel.

## Required Forms

All forms must be complete **before your child's first day**.
Most can be completed electronically in brightwheel.

| Form | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Child's Pre-Admission Health History (LIC 702) | Completed by parent |
| Physician's Report (LIC 701) | Signed by your child's health care provider within the last 12 months |
| Immunization record | Up to date for age, or a valid medical exemption |
| Identification and Emergency Information (LIC 700) | Includes authorized pickup and emergency contacts |
| Consent for Emergency Medical Treatment (LIC 627) | Signed by parent |
| Parents' Rights (LIC 995) | Acknowledgment of receipt |
| Notification of Parents' Rights and Personal Rights (LIC 613A) | Acknowledgment of receipt |
| Enrollment agreement | Signed in brightwheel |
| Photo and media release | Optional; you can choose your preferences |
| Food Allergy Action Plan or Individual Health Care Plan | Only if applicable |
| Infant Needs and Services Plan (LIC 9221) | Seedlings only |

## Gradual Entry

Starting care is a big change.
We recommend a gradual entry over the first week:

- **Day 1:** 2 hours with a parent present
- **Day 2:** 3 hours, parent leaves for part of the visit
- **Day 3:** Half day including lunch
- **Day 4 and 5:** Full day, with an earlier pickup if your child needs it

Your child's primary teacher will check in with you daily during the first two weeks.

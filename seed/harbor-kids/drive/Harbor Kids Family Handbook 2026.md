### Our Mission

To help each child develop independance, concentration, and a lifelong love of learning in a calm, prepared enviroment that respects the child as a capable person.

### What is Montessori?

Montessori education was developed by Dr. Maria Montessori over 100 years ago. In a Montessori classroom children choose their own "work" from carefully prepared materials, work at their own pace, and learn from older and younger classmates in a mixed-age room. Teachers (we call them guides) observe and present lessons to individual children when they are ready.

Key parts of our program:
- **Mixed age classrooms** (18 months-3 years, and 3-6 years)
- **Uninterrupted work cycle** every morning from 9:00 to 11:30
- **Practical life** - pouring, food prep, care of plants, dressing frames
- **Sensorial, language, math, and cultural** materials
- **Outdoor time** every single day, rain or shine
- **Grace and courtesy** lessons to build social skills

## 6. Tours and Enrollment

We offer tours **Tuesdays and Thursdays at 9:30 am**. Call the front desk or use the form in brightwheel to sign up.

After touring, families can submit an application ($100). Siblings and children of staff have priority, then families are offered spots in the order they applied.

Before your child's first day you must turn in the full enrollment packet (see the Enrollment Packet Checklist).

**Phase-in:** New children start with three short days. Toddlers usually take 2-3 weeks to fully settle, which is completely normal.

## 7. Arrival and Pickup

- Check in and out with **brightwheel** every day. This is a licensing requirment.
- Walk your child to the classroom door and say goodbye to a guide. In primary, children hang up their own coat and change into indoor shoes (please send slippers or indoor shoes!).
- Keep goodbyes short and loving. Lingering usually makes it harder.

**Authorized pickup**
- We will only release your child to adults on your brightwheel pickup list.
- Anyone we don't know will be asked for photo ID.
- If there is a parenting plan or court order that limits who can pick up your child, **we must have a copy on file**. Without one we cannot legally refuse a parent.

**Late pickup**
- We close at 6:00. Late fee is $1 per minute after closing.
- If no one has picked up a child 30 minutes after closing and we can't reach parents or emergency contacts, we will call Seattle Police / CPS as required.

## 8. Health and Illness

Please see our **Illness Policy** (in brightwheel and posted in the lobby) for the full list of symptoms that require your child to stay home.

The short version: keep your child home with a fever, vomiting, diarrhea, or a contagious rash, and keep them home until they have been well for 24 hours. We will call you to pick up within one hour if your child gets sick at school.

**Medication** can only be given with a signed Medication Authorization form, in the original container.

**Allergies:** Families must give us an allergy care plan signed by their doctor. The Puffins room is peanut-free because of a child with a severe allergy.

**Immunizations:** Washington requires a Certificate of Immunization Status (CIS) for every child, or a Certificate of Exemption.

## 9. Food

We provide a morning snack, lunch, and an afternoon snack. Lunch is catered by Little Fork Kitchen, a local kid-focused caterer, and snacks are prepared in our classrooms, often by the children themselves (practical life!).

Families may send lunch from home instead. Please let us know so we do not order a lunch for your child. Lunches from home should be nut free for toddlers.

We participate in the USDA food program and follow its meal pattern.

## 11. Clothing

We go outside **every day** unless it is dangerous (lightning, extreme cold, AQI over 150).

Please send:
- Rain jacket and rain pants (or a rain suit for toddlers)
- Rain boots
- Warm hat and gloves in winter
- Two full changes of clothes, labeled
- Indoor shoes or slippers

Clothes that kids can manage themselves (elastic waist pants!) support independence.

## 13. Behavior and Guidance

We use positive, respectful guidance. Guides set clear limits, model grace and courtesy, and help children use words to solve problems. We never use physical punishment, shaming, or withholding food.

Biting is common in toddlers. If your child is bitten or bites, we will tell you the same day. We will never share the name of the other child.

## 14. Safety

- Monthly fire drills and quarterly earthquake drills

# Enrollment Packet Checklist

Harbor Kids Montessori - please return ALL items before your child's first day. Most can be completed in brightwheel. Questions? office.harborkids@example.com

## Required for every child

- [ ] Enrollment agreement (signed in brightwheel)
- [ ] Child registration / emergency information form (parents, emergency contacts, authorized pickups)
- [ ] Certificate of Immunization Status (CIS), or Certificate of Exemption signed by a health care provider
- [ ] Health history form (completed by parent)
- [ ] Health care provider information (name, clinic, phone)
- [ ] Consent for emergency medical treatment
- [ ] Field trip / walking trip permission (annual)
- [ ] Photo and media permission (choose yes or no)
- [ ] Parent handbook acknowledgment
- [ ] Brightwheel autopay set up
- [ ] $200 registration fee paid
- [ ] First month tuition deposit paid

## If applicable

- [ ] Allergy / medical care plan signed by provider (food allergy, asthma, seizures, etc.)
- [ ] Medication authorization form + medication in original container
- [ ] Parenting plan or court order (if there are any restrictions on pickup or information)
- [ ] Working Connections subsidy authorization (DCYF)
- [ ] Special diet request (religious, cultural, medical)
- [ ] IEP / IFSP or therapist contact (if you would like us to coordinate)

## For Puffins (toddlers)

- [ ] Diapering / toilet learning info sheet
- [ ] Sleep and comfort routine sheet
- [ ] 2 packs of diapers + wipes (we'll let you know when to restock)

## Bring on the first day

- Rain suit or rain jacket + rain pants
- Rain boots
- Indoor shoes or slippers
- 2 full changes of clothes, labeled
- Small blanket + comfort item for nap
- Family photo for the classroom family wall

*Note: this checklist was updated June 2026. The older version asked for a TB test for children - that is no longer required.*

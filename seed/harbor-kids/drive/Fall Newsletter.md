# Harbor Kids Fall Newsletter 2026 🍂

Dear Harbor Kids families,

Happy fall! The leaves are turning on Ballard Commons Way and our classrooms are humming. Our new Puffins have settled in beautifully, the Orcas have discovered the joy of polishing (everything), and the Otters kindergarteners are writing their first stories. Here's what's coming up this season.

## Mark Your Calendars

**Pumpkin Patch Field Trip - Friday, October 16**
Orcas and Otters are heading to Fairbanks Farm! The bus leaves at 9:30 and we'll be back by noon for lunch at school. Please send rain boots and a rain jacket. **Permission slips were due October 9** - if you haven't turned yours in, please do so right away or your child will stay at school with the Puffins. Thank you to our parent chaperones!

**Picture Day - Thursday, October 22**
Lumen Kids Photography will be here starting at 8:30 am. Order forms are in brightwheel. Retakes are November 19.

**Harvest Festival - Friday, October 30, 4:00-6:00 pm**
Join us in the play yard for apple cider, a pumpkin-decorating station, music, and a potluck snack table (sign up sheet in the lobby). Costumes are optional - please keep them friendly for our littlest Puffins. Siblings and grandparents welcome!

**Parent-Teacher Conferences - November 10-14**
Conferences are 20 minutes with your child's guide. Sign up in brightwheel. We've added Saturday morning slots on November 14 (9:00 am - noon) for working families.

**Closures**

## From the Classrooms

**Puffins (Toddlers):** We are practicing pouring water from small pitchers, washing our own hands, and putting on our rain boots (mostly!). Our favorite song this month is "Five Little Pumpkins."

**Orcas (Primary):** The Orcas are studying the life cycle of the pumpkin. We'll open one up after the field trip and count the seeds. Children are also doing lots of practical life work: flower arranging, table scrubbing, and food prep.

**Otters (Primary + Kindergarten):** Our kindergarteners are working with the Golden Beads and the Movable Alphabet, and have started a class journal about the salmon run at the Ballard Locks.

## Reminders

- **Rain gear every day.** We go outside in all weather. Label everything!
- **Indoor shoes.** Primary children change into slippers or indoor shoes.
- **Peanut-free Puffins.** Please do not send peanut products in toddler lunches.
- **Illness policy.** Our updated 2026 policy is in brightwheel. Children need to be fever-free (under 100.4°F) for 24 hours without medicine before returning.

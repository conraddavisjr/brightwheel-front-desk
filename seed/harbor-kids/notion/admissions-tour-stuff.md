# Admissions/tour stuff

TOURS - Wednesdays at 10:00am, about 30 min, w/ Gretchen or Marcus. max 3 families. book via the tour form in brightwheel or call the front desk. evening tours 1st thursday of the month 5:45pm for working parents.

(old: tours were Tues/Thurs - we stopped that in 2025, the handbook may still say it)

ages: 18 months to kindergarten (6). must be walking for Puffins.

## waitlist
- $100 application fee nonrefundable
- siblings + staff kids get priority, then by date
- toddler waitlist is long (8-12 mo), primary 3-6 mo, kinder usually has room
- when a spot opens families have 3 days to say yes

## to enroll
1. accept offer
2. pay $200 registration + first month tuition as deposit
3. complete enrollment packet (see Enrollment packet checklist in the drive)
4. phase-in: 3 short days the first week

## Montessori stuff ppl ask
- mixed ages 3-6 in primary
- we use AMI trained leads in Orcas and Otters (Marcus is AMI, Priya is AMS)
- kinder year is the "third year" in primary, extended day 
- not religious
- potty: toddlers work on toilet learning in the Puffins room, no requirement for primary but most are trained

FAQ answer for "how is this different from daycare?" - focus on independence, practical life, child-led work cycle 9-11:30, real materials, mixed ages.

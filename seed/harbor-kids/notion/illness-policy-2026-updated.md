# Illness Policy 2026 UPDATED

**THIS REPLACES THE 2024 POLICY.** Effective August 2026, per our updated health consultant review and WAC 110-300 child care licensing rules.

## Keep your child home if:

### Fever
- Temp of **100.4°F or higher**.
- Your child can come back once they have been **fever-free for 24 hours WITHOUT fever reducing medicine** (Tylenol, Motrin, etc). Giving meds in the morning to get through drop off is NOT ok, we will send them home.

### Vomiting
- 2 or more times in 24 hours. Back after 24 hours with no vomiting.

### Diarrhea
- 3 or more loose stools in 24 hrs, or any that leaks out of the diaper. Back after 24 hours symptom free.

### Pink eye
- Yellow/green discharge. Back once seen by a doctor and treatment started (no more 24hr wait).

### Rash
- Rash + fever, or spreading rash. Back with doctor's note.

### Lice
- We no longer have a no-nit policy. Child can return after first treatment.

### COVID / Flu / RSV
- Follow current Washington DOH guidance. We'll post updates in brightwheel.

## Sending kids home
If your child gets sick at school we'll call you and you need to pick up within **1 hour**. Child waits in the office with a staff member.

## Medications
- Prescription AND over the counter meds can be given with a signed **Medication Authorization** form + original container.
- OTC meds need written instructions from your child's provider if the dose is different from the label.
- No first doses at school.
- Kids with an epi-pen or inhaler must have a care plan signed by their doctor. Epi-pens go everywhere the class goes.

## Notifying us
Mark your child absent in brightwheel and tell us the symptoms so we can track illnesses and notify the class (we never share names).

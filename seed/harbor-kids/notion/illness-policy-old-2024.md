# Illness Policy

*adopted Aug 2024*

Harbor Kids follows public health guidance to keep everyone well. Please keep your child at home if they are sick.

**Fever:** Children with a temperature of 101 degrees F or higher will be sent home. Your child may come back **24 hours after their fever goes below 101**.

**Vomiting / diarrhea:** Children who vomit or have diarrhea at school will be sent home. Please keep your child home until symptoms have stopped for 24 hours.

**Pink eye:** Child may return after 24 hours on antibiotic drops.

**Colds:** A runny nose by itself is fine as long as your child is comfortable and can participate. Green discharge with fever = stay home.

**COVID-19:** Please test at home if symptomatic. Positive cases must stay home 5 days and mask for 5 days after returning (kids over 2).

**Lice:** No-nit policy. Child cannot return until all nits are removed.

**Pickup:** Parents must pick up a sick child within 1 hour of being called.

**Medications:** We can give prescription meds with a signed form. We do not give OTC meds (Tylenol, Motrin, etc.) at all.
